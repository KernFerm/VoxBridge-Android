Comfort is a feature

Adjust the experience, not yourself.

Settings are grouped by purpose and explained in plain language. Increase text size, strengthen contrast, reduce motion, or simplify controls without digging through a crowded menu.

  • Optional history is off by default
  • Favorites stay on your device

Privacy without fine print fog

Your words are not advertising material.

VoxBridge has no advertising, analytics, user accounts, or location tracking. Online translation sends the text you choose to the selected service; on-device methods keep translation text on your phone after model download.
