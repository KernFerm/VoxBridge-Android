VoxBridge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

VoxBridge does not include advertising, analytics, user accounts, or location tracking. The app requests internet access but does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or location permission.

Translation text

When you choose Microsoft online translation, the text you submit is sent over encrypted HTTPS to the private VoxBridge server and then to Microsoft Translator to return the requested translation. The VoxBridge server does not store the submitted text in its quota records.

When you choose Googletrans online translation, the text you submit is sent to Google Translate's web service to return the requested translation.

When you choose Google ML Kit, OPUS-MT, or M2M100, translation runs on your device after required model files are downloaded. Google or Hugging Face may receive ordinary model-download requests, but the text you translate is not included in those model downloads.

Installation identifier and service protection

VoxBridge generates a random installation identifier for the Microsoft online translation service. The server combines service metadata into a one-way hash and stores the hash with usage counters to enforce shared limits, prevent abuse, and keep the service available. This identifier is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Speech

Android text-to-speech is the production speech method. Speech behavior depends on the speech engine and voices installed on your device. An installed speech engine may have its own network and privacy practices.

Information stored on your device

Favorites, settings, and optional history are stored locally. History is disabled by default. You can delete history, favorites, settings, and downloaded model caches from the app. Clearing the app's Android storage or uninstalling the app also removes its local data. Exported files are controlled by you after export.

Payments

Google Play processes purchases and payment details. VoxBridge does not receive or store your card information.

Children

VoxBridge is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use of any online translation service.

Security and retention

Online requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Translation text passing through the VoxBridge server is used to fulfill the request and is not written to the app's quota database. Pseudonymous quota hashes and counters are retained only as needed to operate and protect the shared translation service.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
